Leave balances for everyone in YouTrack, synced from Microsoft Teams Shifts.

Employees request time off in Microsoft Teams Shifts as usual. Leave Sync for YouTrack reads the approved time off and adds a Leaves page to the YouTrack main menu. There, every user sees their own remaining leave, their usage by leave type and their leave history. Administrators see the whole team in one table and manage hire dates, entitlements and holidays in YouTrack.

For employees

  • Remaining annual leave in days and hours, with this year's entitlement, the balance carried over and the leave used.
  • Usage by leave type (for example vacation, sick day or parental leave) for a leave year, a calendar year or any custom date range.
  • Leave history including approved upcoming leave, with corrections and notes from the manager.
  • Users only ever see their own data. This is enforced on the server.

For administrators

  • Team overview: remaining leave, annual leave used, other leave and a column per leave type for every employee, with team totals for any period.
  • Open any employee's page exactly as they see it.
  • Seniority-based entitlement granted automatically on each work anniversary, with carry-over of unused leave and an optional age rule. Every value can be overridden per person and per year.
  • Easy onboarding: enter how many days a person has left today, and the opening balance is calculated for you.
  • Leave types from Teams: choose which time-off reasons are deducted from annual leave. Others are only counted.
  • Corrections: changes made in the Shifts schedule count right away, even before they are shared. Any single leave can also be corrected in YouTrack, with a required note.
  • Change history of every administrative change.

How leave is counted

  • Leave is tracked in hours: partial-day and hourly leave are counted exactly.
  • Weekends and public holidays are never deducted. Half-day holidays count as half a day.
  • Company time zone with optional EU summer time.
  • The working hours per day, the non-working days and the seniority tiers are configurable. The default entitlement tiers follow Turkish labour law (14 / 20+ / 26 days).

Requirements

  • YouTrack 2026.2 or later (Cloud or Server).
  • Microsoft Teams with Shifts used for time-off requests.
  • A Microsoft Entra app registration with the Schedule.Read.All and User.Read.All application permissions. The Getting Started section shows each step.

Try it without Microsoft 365

Tick Use demo data in the app settings to try every feature with sample Teams Shifts data generated for the employees you add. No Microsoft account is needed and no data leaves YouTrack. Untick it to switch to your real Teams data; nothing is deleted.

Data and privacy

  • The app connects only to Microsoft: login.microsoftonline.com to sign in with your app registration, and graph.microsoft.com to read time off, time-off reasons and the user e-mail addresses needed to match Teams users to YouTrack users. It never writes to Microsoft 365.
  • Microsoft is the only third party involved: requests to Microsoft contain your app registration credentials, team IDs and Teams user IDs. No YouTrack data is sent to Microsoft.
  • Leave data, hire dates and balances are stored in your YouTrack instance only. Nothing is sent to the app vendor, and there is no telemetry or analytics.
  • The client secret is stored as a YouTrack secret setting and is never sent to the browser.
